Coeo

Coeo brings a fresh approach to hostels and holiday apartments, built around the people you meet, the experiences you share, and the moments you take home. We helped turn that idea into a distinctive voice, bringing it to life across the website, guest journeys, social media, and physical spaces

My role

I helped research and develop Coeo’s voice, then translated it into copy for the website, guest journeys, social media, and physical spaces. Along the way, we explored how words could express the idea of human connection - sparking curiosity before arrival and creating a sense of belonging from the very first moment

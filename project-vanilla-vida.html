Vanilla Vida

Vanilla Vida brings science and technology to traditional vanilla farming. I helped shape its story and voice for a food industry eager for innovation, yet cautious about change - making the business value clear while keeping the romance of vanilla alive

My role

I helped research and develop Vanilla Vida’s story and voice, then translated them into website copy. The writing connected complex science to customer benefits - from new flavors and aromas to improvements across the supply chain - in a way that sparked both imagination and culinary inspiration

The challenge

We were writing for people curious about new flavors, but cautious about new suppliers. In an industry that depends on reliable ingredients and regulatory compliance, an intriguing promise is only the beginning. We had to balance imagination with clear explanations and concrete information

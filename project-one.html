Riseup

How to talk about money with clarity, empathy and no judgment - and help people change their habits

The task

Develop and refine Riseup’s emerging voice, turning it into clear principles that guide writing across the product, customer support and marketing. Create a consistent voice that makes financial topics easier to understand and helps people feel capable and confident enough to take action.

My role

Together with the Riseup team, we ran voice workshops, mapped customer journeys and explored the challenges of everyday customer support conversations. From there, the voice principles took shape in product microcopy and conversations with customers - helping people understand how it works, imagine what’s possible and take action.

Onboarding: the start of a relationship

Onboarding was an opportunity to introduce users to both Riseup’s approach and the kind of relationship they could expect with it. Drawing on the voice principles I helped define, we combined an explanation of cash flow with messages of personal connection, empowerment and ongoing support. It was important for users to understand how the service works and feel they had a partner along the way.
